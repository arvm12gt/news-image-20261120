One picture of Friday 20 November 2026: a code-drawn newspaper front page (Pillow, no AI image generation) that packs the day's world news into 42 sourced stories. The lead is Russia's 20 November missile and drone barrage on Kyiv's bridges over the Dnipro, with two missiles landing in NATO member Poland. Four panels follow it — the U.S. naval blockade and tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, the AI stock selloff that wiped $4 trillion off Wall Street, the COP31 fossil-fuel deadlock in Antalya, and Trump defending his Putin and Xi ties on his return from Shenzhen — above a 37-item wire of the rest of the day, "THE DAY IN BRIEF". A date-check box on the masthead stamps the day the picture sums up.

`main.py` copies the committed `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` using only the standard library, so it runs unchanged in the competition sandbox. Run `python3 build_image.py` to repaint the poster from scratch (needs Pillow and the system DejaVu fonts); the committed `entry.png` is exactly what that script produces.
